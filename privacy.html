Privacy Policy

Effective 18 August 2026. This policy explains what data geminiwatermark.space processes when you use the watermark-removal tool and the ₹99 video plan.

Media processing

Images and videos selected in the remover are processed in your browser by the client-side processing engine. The site is designed not to upload or store your selected media files on our payment backend.

Payment and account data

When you buy the ₹99 plan, the mobile number and optional email you enter, along with order and payment metadata, are sent to our server and Cashfree as needed to create and verify the payment. Cashfree may process additional payment information under its own privacy terms.

Local browser storage

The site uses local storage to remember the free-video usage counter and a signed plan token. The token is used to check whether your 30-day paid access is active and to display purchase details in My Account. You can remove the saved account token from My Account at any time.

Analytics

Vercel Web Analytics may be used to understand aggregate traffic such as visits, referrers, countries and device types. Analytics is used to improve site performance and discoverability.

Retention and security

Payment records are retained by the payment provider and related systems as required for transaction support, reconciliation, fraud prevention and legal obligations. We use signed entitlement tokens and server-side payment verification so a client cannot unlock paid access by changing browser values alone.

Your choices

  • You may use free image processing without buying a plan.
  • Email is optional during checkout unless a payment flow requires it.
  • You can clear local storage or use the My Account removal control to remove the local signed token from your browser.
