How to remove a Gemini video watermark without cropping.

Keep your portrait composition intact while cleaning a supported visible mark from a Gemini or Veo video export.

Aug 23, 2026•5 min read

Cropping is tempting because many visible marks sit near an edge. But for a 9:16 clip, even a small crop can shift the subject, reduce resolution and force another resize before posting.

Preserve the original 9:16 canvas

If your source is 1080×1920, keep it at 1080×1920 through the cleanup step. The same principle applies to 720×1280 and other portrait exports. A watermark profile depends on where the mark sits in the frame.

Use targeted cleanup instead of zooming

A local visible-watermark workflow edits only the supported mark region instead of changing the entire frame. This is especially useful for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and story formats where edge composition matters.

Choose the right video profile

Recent Gemini exports and older Veo layouts can differ. Use the Gemini video watermark remover for supported recent profiles and the Veo watermark remover for supported legacy layouts.

Check moving textures carefully

Hair, clothing, leaves, text and sharp lines can reveal a weak cleanup more easily than flat sky or walls. Preview several moments in the clip before saving the final result.
