GEMINI & FLOW UPDATE · 2026

How to turn off or avoid a Gemini visible watermark in 2026

There is not one universal watermark toggle for every Gemini or Google Flow user. Current visible-watermark behavior depends on the product, Google One tier and local regulations, while invisible SynthID remains separate.

Updated Aug 20, 2026•6 min read

Can you turn off the visible Gemini or Flow watermark?

For Google Flow video, the visible watermark depends on your plan and region rather than one universal on/off setting. Google says videos generated on Free, Plus and Pro tiers can contain a visible watermark. Ultra outputs may avoid the visible watermark, except when local regulations require one.

Current Flow ruleAll Flow outputs generated with Veo and Imagen include invisible SynthID. Visible watermark rules are separate from that provenance signal.

Why a visible watermark can still appear

  • Plan tier: visible watermark behavior differs across Google One tiers.
  • Local regulations: Ultra users can still receive a visible watermark where regulations require it.
  • Wrong Google account: Flow may be open under a different account from the account that owns your paid plan.
  • Old export: a file already saved with the visible mark does not change when your plan or account changes later.

If you expected no visible mark and it is still appearing, use the Gemini / Flow watermark still showing troubleshooting guide.

Does this remove SynthID?

No. A visible logo and SynthID are different. The visible watermark is the mark you can see in the frame. SynthID is an invisible provenance signal designed to help identify AI-generated content.

Getting an export without a visible logo does not mean the media has lost its AI provenance information.

What happens to files you already exported?

If an image or video is already saved on your device with the visible mark baked into the pixels, changing plan, account or generation conditions later does not rewrite that existing file.

For an already-exported file that you own or are authorized to edit, a visible-watermark cleanup workflow can still be useful. The browser tool on geminiwatermark.space targets supported visible Gemini and Veo watermark profiles while leaving SynthID outside its scope.

Gemini image watermark: new generation vs old export

  • New generation: check the current product and account behavior before creating the final asset.
  • Already exported image: the visible mark may already be part of the saved pixels.
  • SynthID: remains a separate invisible provenance mechanism.
  • Publishing: follow the platform, client and regional disclosure rules that apply to your use case.

Gemini and Veo video watermark workflow

For videos, keep the original export whenever possible. Re-encoding, cropping and editing can change the watermark location and make later cleanup less predictable. Recent 9:16 story and reel exports commonly use portrait dimensions such as 1080×1920, so the original source is normally the best file to work with.

If your Flow account produces an export without a visible watermark under your current tier and region, use that direct export for future clips. If you are working with an older exported clip that still contains a supported visible mark, use the Gemini video watermark remover guide.
