PRICING GUIDE

What Gemini watermark cleanup costs.

What you pay, why video is heavier work than images, and what a browser tool can realistically do.

Image and video cleanup have very different processing requirements. A single image is one frame; a video can contain hundreds or thousands of frames plus audio and encoding work.

Both image and video cleanup are free

The Gemini image watermark remover and the video remover are currently free to use. There is no account, no card and no subscription.

Why video is still the harder job

Cost is not the only difference. A still image is one repair; a video repeats that repair across every frame, then re-encodes the result. That is why video takes longer, uses far more of your device, and is more sensitive to the source you start from.

Check the file before you process it

You do not have to commit before you know your export is supported. Select the file first and the format, dimensions and likely watermark profile are checked on your device, so an unsupported source fails immediately instead of halfway through.

What no tool can do

A visible-watermark workflow reconstructs the covered area from surrounding pixels, so busy motion can leave artifacts. It also does not touch SynthID or other invisible provenance signals — see visible watermark vs SynthID.

Try your file first

Choose image or video mode and select the source to see whether it is supported.
