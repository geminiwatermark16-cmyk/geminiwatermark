Does Gemini watermark removal remove SynthID?

No. Removing a supported visible overlay is different from removing invisible provenance technology.

A visible watermark is an on-screen logo or mark that a viewer can see. SynthID is designed as an invisible signal embedded in AI-generated media. They are separate layers.

Visible cleanup changes pixels you can see

A visible-watermark remover targets a known logo or overlay region and reconstructs the surrounding image or video area.

SynthID is not the target

geminiwatermark.space does not claim to remove SynthID or other hidden provenance signals. The tool is limited to supported visible watermark profiles.

Why this distinction matters

A clean-looking export can still contain AI provenance information. Visible appearance and provenance are not the same thing.

For a deeper explanation, read Visible Watermark vs SynthID.
