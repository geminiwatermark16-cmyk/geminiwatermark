Best video format for Gemini watermark removal.

MP4 vs WebM vs MOV: the best choice is usually the original source file, not a converted copy.

MP4: widest compatibility

MP4 is easy to play and publish across most devices and platforms. If your original Gemini or Flow export is MP4, keep it as the cleanup source.

WebM: strong browser workflow

WebM works well in modern browsers and can preserve a direct browser-generated export. Do not convert it just because MP4 is more familiar.

MOV: common on Apple workflows

MOV can be an excellent source when it is the original export. Browser codec support can vary, so use the upload-first step to confirm the file loads.

Which format gives the best quality?

The most important factor is avoiding unnecessary recompression. An original WebM can be better than a twice-converted MP4, and an original MOV can be better than a social-media download.

Convert after cleanup only when needed

If your publishing platform requires another format, clean the highest-quality source first and make one final conversion afterward.
