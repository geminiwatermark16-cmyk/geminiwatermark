PROFILE GUIDE

New Gemini diamond vs old Veo watermark.

Choosing the correct visible watermark profile is one of the most important steps in video cleanup.

A cleanup profile is not universal. Different generations of Google AI video exports can place different visible marks in different locations.

Newer Gemini diamond profile

Recent supported exports may use a small Gemini-style diamond mark. The Gemini video watermark remover includes guidance for this newer layout.

Legacy Veo profile

Older supported Veo exports can use a different visible text or logo layout. Those files should use the legacy Veo workflow.

Why the wrong profile fails

If the tool edits the wrong coordinates, the original mark can remain while another part of the frame is unnecessarily altered.

Check resolution too

Even the correct watermark type can be misaligned after resizing. Always keep the original frame dimensions when possible.
