Gemini video watermark remover for WebM files.

A browser-first workflow for supported visible watermark cleanup on WebM video exports.

WebM is common in browser-based AI video workflows. If your Gemini or Veo export is already WebM, keep the original file instead of converting it before cleanup.

Why WebM can be a good source

Modern browsers handle WebM efficiently, and keeping the original avoids another compression pass. That preserves detail around the visible watermark region.

Do not rename WebM to MP4

Changing only the filename extension does not change the codec. If conversion is needed later, use a real encoder after cleanup rather than before it.

Check resolution before processing

Portrait and landscape files need matching profiles. Use the Gemini video watermark remover and keep native dimensions intact.
